QR BOOST (Kabaddi setting · QR pops vs ground)

purposeMake A3 QR B/W pop more than green/brown ground at 10/15 m
how it worksHigh contrast 1.8-2.0 makes black/white QR pop vs mid-tone ground, low sat 0.5-0.7 desaturates green grass/brown dirt, high sharp 2.0+ makes finder patterns crisp at 15 m, slightly dark -10 avoids washout, manual exp 5 ms reduces blur.

Kabaddi setting: QR is on ground, so ground is noise. ISP: contrast HIGH (1.8-2.0) → QR B/W contrast vs ground, saturation LOW (0.5-0.7) → green/brown grass desaturated to gray, QR B/W stays, sharpness HIGH (2.0) → finder patterns crisp at 10/15 m, brightness -10 → white ground not blown, exposure manual 5 ms → less motion blur. Software: CLAHE on L channel + unsharp mask + HSV green/brown mask desat 40% + V boost. See docs/arena_test1/00_DETECTION... for pixel budget.
